Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the school timings for Grades 1–10?

School runs from 08:30 AM to 03:30 PM for Grades 1–10. Office desks are accessible from 08:00 AM to 04:00 PM for admissions inquiries.

How can I book a physical campus tour?

You can book a visit directly by selecting "Book a Campus Visit" in the inquiry form, or call our admissions counsellor at +91 78880 56699.

What documents are required during registration?

We require the child's birth certificate, school leaving certificate (from previous school), past progress reports, Aadhaar card copy, and passport-size photographs.
