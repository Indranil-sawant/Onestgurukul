Curriculum Map

The Academic Learning Journey

An age-appropriate learning curriculum that transitions students smoothly from basic foundation to future readiness.

Grades 1–2

Foundation Stage

Developing early communication skills, phonics, basic arithmetic values, and hands-on sensorimotor play classes.

Grades 3–5

Exploration Stage

Introduction to scientific concepts, social environments, grammar, complex mathematics, and active art workshops.

Grades 6–8

Skill Development Stages

Empowering coding labs, advanced experimental sciences, critical essay writing, team sports, and public speaking forums.

Grades 9–10

Future Preparation

Robust board curriculum preparation, personality development modules, career counselling guidance, and advanced competitive Olympiad preparations.
