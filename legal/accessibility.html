ACCESSIBILITY COMPLIANCE STATUS

O'Nest Gurukul is committed to facilitating web accessibility for all visitors. This statement highlights our current implementation and known technical limitations. It does not constitute a certified guarantee of full WCAG compliance.

1. Commitment to Accessibility

O'Nest Gurukul is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone, and applying the relevant accessibility standards to make our school portal as inclusive as possible.

2. Actions Taken & WCAG Principles

During our pre-delivery audit, the following adjustments were implemented to align with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.1 Level AA):

  • Form Associations: All input elements in the admissions forms, lead forms, and contact forms have been matched with corresponding, screen-reader-friendly `
  • Text Equivalents: Descriptive `alt` attributes have been added to all campus facilities and pre-primary gallery photographs. Decorative graphics are marked with empty alt strings to prevent screen-reader clutter.
  • Markup Structure: Standardized heading hierarchy (`h1`, `h2`, `h3`) across all pages for consistent document flows during keyboard navigation.
  • Interactive Toggles: Accordions and drawers feature clear visual focus indicators.

3. Known Accessibility Limitations

While we strive to ensure absolute accessibility, some elements on our website depend on third-party frameworks and may have limitations:

  • Interactive Maps: The embedded Google Map coordinates frame on `contact.html` might have limitations regarding keyboard-only panning controls. We provide a direct "Open Maps" redirect link as an accessible alternative.
  • Theme Toggle Transitions: The light/dark styling transitions compilation on budget mobile browsers may cause slight layout reflow latency.
  • External Media: Circular and announcement PDFs uploaded to the download hub are supplied in official scanned formats which may not be fully optimized for screen-reader engines.
