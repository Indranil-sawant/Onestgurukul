### PREAMBLE
This Agreement defines the division of rights, intellectual property ownership, technical maintenance scope, and operational boundaries concerning the official website of O'Nest Gurukul deployed at `https://onestgurukul.in/`.

---

#### 1. Ownership of Deliverables & Materials
1. **School-Owned Materials:**  
   The School retains absolute, unencumbered ownership of all institution-specific content, trademarks, logos, brand assets, student/faculty photographs, written academic curricula, policy texts, and proprietary school data provided for inclusion on the website.
2. **Developer-Owned & Reusable Components:**  
   The Developer retains ownership of pre-existing software routines, generic design frameworks, reusable layout components, and utility libraries. The Developer grants the School an irrevocable, non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free license to use, host, display, and modify such code for the operation of the School's website.
3. **Third-Party & Open Source Assets:**  
   All third-party libraries (e.g., Tailwind CSS, Bootstrap Icons, Google Fonts, Supabase JS) remain subject to their respective open-source licenses (MIT, SIL Open Font License, Apache 2.0). Neither party claims proprietary rights over third-party open-source dependencies.

---

#### 2. Division of Operational Responsibilities
1. **Content & Legal Regulatory Compliance (The School):**  
   The School is exclusively responsible for the veracity, factual accuracy, legal legality, and parental consent of all notices, circulars, fee disclosures, admission policies, and student images published on the website.
2. **Technical Engineering & Architecture (The Developer):**  
   The Developer is responsible for delivering functional, clean, accessible, mobile-responsive HTML/CSS/JS code, configuring deployment pipelines (GitHub Actions, custom domain CNAME), integrating webhooks (Google Apps Script), and implementing client-side performance optimizations.
3. **No Ongoing Fiduciary / Operational Duty:**  
   The Developer does not operate as an admissions officer, legal counsel, or student data custodian. The Developer shall not be liable for school admission disputes, administrative communications, or regulatory school accreditation requirements.

---

#### 3. Privacy, Confidentiality, & Child Protection
1. **Confidentiality:** The Developer agrees to keep confidential all internal administrative credentials, parent enquiry sheets, and student records accessed during testing.
2. **No Secret Backdoors:** The Developer warrants that the codebase contains no malicious telemetry, unauthorized trackers, or undisclosed backdoors.
3. **Key Safeguards:** The Developer shall never commit private API keys (e.g., Supabase `service_role` keys or email service credentials) to public Git repositories.
