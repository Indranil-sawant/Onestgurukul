Curriculum Pathway

The 4 Foundational Stages

Our curriculum adapts to each child’s developmental milestones, transitioning seamlessly from joyful sensory play to confident academic readiness.

Age 1.5 – 2.5 Yrs

Playgroup

Gentle separation, sensory block exploration, tactile basin sorting, and social comfort in a warm, loving playroom.

Sensory Logic Social Bonds
Age 2.5 – 3.5 Yrs

Nursery

Speech enrichment, Vedic shlokas, sand & rangoli craft activities, music rhythm circles, and color recognition.

Fine Motor Speech & Rhymes
Age 3.5 – 4.5 Yrs

Junior KG

Montessori shape-peg boards, early phonics & pre-writing grip, number identification, and collaborative play.

Phonics Basis Number Sense
Age 4.5 – 5.5 Yrs

Senior KG

Early reading fluency, experiential math, environmental exploration, stage confidence, and primary grade readiness.

Reading Fluency Primary Ready

Thoughtfully Designed Spaces

Sunlit, Acoustic & Sensory Classrooms.

Every room is designed to reduce sensory overload while encouraging joyful physical activity, balance, and fine motor dexterity.

☁️

Acoustic Cloud Ceiling

Acoustic cloud baffles absorb ambient sound, creating a calm, echo-free atmosphere for toddlers.

🛝

Indoor Gross Motor Slide

Soft-landing indoor physical play equipment supporting balance and body coordination.

🧩

Low-Reach Montessori Organizers

Children access tactile blocks, rings, and puzzles independently, cultivating self-discipline.

📋

Daily Milestone Activity Board

Structured learning outcomes including 'Ball Rolling on Path', eye-tracking, and grip development.

Preschool Adventures

Why Children Love Our School

We design spaces and modules specifically for early childhood wonders.

Learning Through Play

Developing sensory logic via tactile grains sorting, number balloons, and group puzzle sessions.

Music & Shlokas

Morning prayer shlokas, singing circles, rhythmic nursery rhymes, and joyful coordination dances.

Storytelling & Plays

Fancy dress character days, little Shivaji Maharaj pageants, and story castle sessions with large props.

Creative Art & Craft

Diya painting with parents, finger art, sand rangoli craft, and sensory textures that refine motor grip.

Adventure Map

A Day Full of Adventure

Our day is structured as a playful journey across developmental milestones.

Step 01

Sunrise Welcome

A warm hug from teachers, soft music, and settling into individual activity blocks.

Step 02

Circle Time & Shlokas

Interactive greetings, morning Vedic shlokas, and rhythmic rhymes in loving circles.

Step 03

Snack Time & Manners

Eating healthy food alongside friends while learning polite sharing and dining etiquette.

Step 04

Outdoor Jungle Play

Sandpit exploration, swings, balancing walks, and sensory nature games.

Child Safe Zone

Safety & Loving Care

A completely childproof and sanitised school campus with constant security measures.

Childproof Spaces

Anti-slip play flooring, rounded wall corners, socket protectors, and non-toxic sanitized toys cleaned twice daily.

Live CCTV Monitoring

Continuous CCTV coverage across all learning playrooms, corridors, sandpits, dining spaces, and transport drop-offs.

Dedicated Attendants

Loving female attendants and trained nannies assist with bathroom routines, handwashing, meals, and comfort.
