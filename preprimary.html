Our day is structured as a playful journey across developmental milestones.

Step 01

Sunrise Welcome

A warm hug from teachers, soft music, and settling into activity blocks.

Step 02

Circle Time

Interactive greetings, morning stories, and rhythmic rhymes in groups.

Step 03

Snack Time

Eating healthy food alongside friends while learning table manners.
