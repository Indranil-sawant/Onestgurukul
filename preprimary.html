Age Groups & Safety

Our Pre-Primary section consists of three levels designed to suit the developmental stages of young learners:
Nursery (3–4 years)  |  Junior KG (4–5 years)  |  Senior KG (5–6 years)

Constant Supervision

We prioritize child safety through constant supervision by trained staff to ensure every child is attended to.

Safe Environment

Safe, non-toxic materials and child-proofed classrooms create a secure space for worry-free exploration.

CCTV Surveillance

Comprehensive CCTV surveillance on campus ensures continuous monitoring and enhanced security.

Hygiene & Staff

Strict hygiene protocols and verified support staff maintain a clean, healthy, and trustworthy environment.

Learning Areas

We focus on all-round development through key learning domains, ensuring a balanced and enriching foundation.

Language Development

Phonics, vocabulary building, and communication skills to foster expression and early literacy.

Numeracy Skills

Learning counting, patterns, shapes, and basic math concepts through interactive play and manipulatives.

Motor Skills

Fine motor for writing readiness and craft; Gross motor for running, jumping, coordination, and balance.

Social & Emotional

Developing sharing, teamwork, and confidence building through collaborative activities and emotional guidance.

Joyful Activities

Storytelling, puppet shows, role play, music, dance, and rhythm activities for creative expression.

Exploration & STEAM

Sensory play, hands-on exploration, and simple STEAM-based experiments to spark curiosity.

Teaching Philosophy

We follow a child-centred, play-based learning approach focusing on exploration, creativity, and holistic development.

01

Hands-on Experiences

Learning through active participation and hands-on experiences that make concepts real and memorable.

02

Curiosity & Independence

Encouraging young minds to ask questions, explore ideas, and build self-reliance and confidence.

03

Collaborative Learning

Developing social and emotional skills through teamwork, sharing, and peer interaction.

Facilities Available

Our environment supports safe and enriched learning with facilities designed for young children.

Enriched Classrooms

Age-appropriate classrooms and learning materials designed to stimulate young minds.

Indoor Play Areas

Safe and engaging indoor play areas where children can have fun regardless of the weather.

Activity Corners

Dedicated corners for reading, blocks, art, and pretend play to explore different interests.

Child-Friendly Washrooms

Clean, accessible, and child-friendly washrooms designed for comfort and hygiene.

A Day at Pre-Primary

Structured yet flexible daily routines that balance learning, play, rest, and nutrition for holistic development.

Morning

Circle Time

Greetings, conversations, and introduction to the day’s theme to start the day with positivity.

Learning

Concept Time

Focused activities on early literacy, numeracy, and theme-based learning.

Nutrition

Snack Break

Healthy snacks to recharge energy and social interaction practicing table manners.

Creativity

Art & Craft / Activity Time

Hands-on crafts and activities for fine motor skills and creative expression.

Movement

Gross Motor Activities

Physical activities like running, jumping, balance, and coordination exercises.

Rhythm

Music & Dance

Movement, songs, and rhythm to develop coordination and auditory skills.

Storytelling

Storytelling / Rhymes

Engaging stories and rhymes that spark imagination and language development.

Ending

Closing Circle & Goodbye

Reflecting on the day, sharing appreciation, and singing goodbye songs.

Moments of Joy & Discovery
