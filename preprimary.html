Answers for Parents

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the age criteria for pre-primary admission?

We admit children into Nursery at 2.5 to 3 years of age. Junior KG starts at 3.5 to 4 years, and Senior KG at 4.5 to 5 years, aligned with state board guidelines.

Is transport tracking available for nursery children?

Yes, all school buses are mapped with GPS devices. Parents can track transport vehicles live via our school mobile app and receive notifications.

How often are classrooms sanitized?

Classrooms, play areas, blocks, and reading mats are vacuumed and sanitized twice daily with child-safe, non-toxic sanitizing sprays.
