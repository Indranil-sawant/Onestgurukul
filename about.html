Our Philosophy & Story

01

Founding Purpose

ONEST Gurukul was envisioned as a space where modern CBSE academic conceptualisation meets classical ethical integrity, ensuring a balanced growth track for children.

02

Growth & Evolution

Over the decade, we grew our infrastructure with technology labs, smart boards, vast library archives, and customized child security measures.

03

Future Vision

Expanding into advanced STEM courses, AI concepts, and public speech guidelines, preparing our class 9–10 students for boarding selections.

Safety & Well-being

Our campus prioritizes safety and physical comfort above all else, ensuring parents enjoy complete peace of mind.

CCTV Cameras

24/7 high-definition monitoring active across classrooms, labs, playgrounds, corridors, and campus entrances.

GPS Bus Tracking

Safe transport fleet equipped with operational speed governors, seatbelts, alert systems, and live GPS tracking.

Medical Support

Trained first-aid providers active on campus, coupled with a well-maintained medical station and emergency links.

Secure Campus

Guarded security access checkpoints with restricted visitor protocols, ensuring only verified entries are allowed.
