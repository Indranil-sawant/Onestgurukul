Frequently Asked Questions

When is the last date to apply?

expand_more
Applications for the upcoming academic session remain open until March 31st, subject to seat availability in each grade.

Is there a sibling discount?

expand_more
Yes, we offer a 10% waiver on tuition fees for the younger sibling currently enrolled at Onest Gurukul.

Do you offer boarding facilities?

expand_more
Currently, we are a day-boarding school. However, we offer extended day programs for students involved in specialized extracurricular activities.
