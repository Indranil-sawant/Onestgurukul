Admission Process

A simple, transparent process designed with your child's best interests in mind.

1

Submit Application

Fill out the online application form or visit our campus to submit a physical form along with required documents.

2

Interaction & Assessment

A friendly interaction with the child and parents to understand learning needs and expectations.

3

Document Verification

Submit birth certificate, previous school records, address proof, and passport-size photographs.

4

Admission Confirmation

Receive admission offer, complete fee payment, and confirm enrollment to secure your child's seat.

Eligibility Criteria

We welcome students who are eager to learn and grow in a supportive, value-driven environment.

Age Requirements

Minimum age: 3 years for Nursery, as per government guidelines for each grade level.

Required Documents

Birth certificate, Aadhar card, previous school records, address proof, and recent photographs.

Transfer Students

Transfer certificate from previous school and mark sheets for the last attended grade.
